About the workshop

DBQC brings together academia and industry to ask how quantum computing and data management can enhance each other.

The goal of the workshop is to give an opportunity to local and interantional reseachers, both from academia and industry, to share perspectives on the topics of databases and quantum computing. As quantum hardware is maturing quickly, challenges and opportunities arise at the intersection of these two fields. Quantum computation can improve data management, and tailored databases and data access are necessary for quantum computers to operate.
