About the workshop

QDB brings together researchers in quantum computing and data management to ask what each field can do for the other.

Quantum hardware is maturing quickly, and many of the problems databases face (join ordering, index selection, transaction scheduling) are hard optimisation problems that quantum methods may help with. In the other direction, quantum experiments produce data that classical systems struggle to store and query.

The workshop is a day of talks, with no papers or proceedings. To give a talk, register it by 30 April 2027 with a title and a short abstract.
